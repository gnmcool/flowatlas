## 🎙️ TIPS FOR RECORDING

- **Screen record on your phone** while slowly scrolling each tab — smooth, slow swipes look professional
- **Add lo-fi / market ambience background music** at low volume
- **Use CapCut or Instagram's text tool** to add the spoken words as on-screen captions
- **Record in landscape first** if showing the desktop version, then crop to 9:16 for Reels
- **Best posting times for finance content:** 7–9 AM IST or 8–10 PM IST
- **Post Reels 2–3 days apart** — don't dump all at once
- **Pin Reel 1 (Overall Intro)** to your profile grid
